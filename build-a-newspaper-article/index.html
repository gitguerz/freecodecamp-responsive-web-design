Local Developer Opens 47 Tabs, Calls It Research

One missing semicolon. Three cold coffees. Absolutely no suspects.

By Guerz · Chief Procrastination Correspondent

Witnesses report that a local developer sat down to fix one tiny CSS issue and resurfaced four hours later with a new color palette, strong opinions about fonts, and the original problem completely untouched. “I am building a workflow,” the developer explained, opening an eighth tutorial on productivity. The workflow could not be reached for comment.

The breakthrough arrived when the developer discovered that the stylesheet was never linked. Experts described the incident as “deeply avoidable” and “basically Tuesday.” A nearby rubber duck has requested a raise. At press time, the page finally looked correct, prompting the developer to celebrate by changing everything.
